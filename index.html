INFORMS Annual Meeting 2026 Preview

QSR returns to the INFORMS Annual Meeting this fall in San Francisco, where more than 6,000 members, students, and practitioners will gather around the theme Smarter Decisions for a Better World.

Dates
November 1–4, 2026
Location
San Francisco, CA
Venue
Moscone Center South & Marriott Marquis

Basic conference information

The 2026 meeting is fully in person, with technical sessions running Sunday through Wednesday. The QSR Section Poster Session takes place on Monday, November 2, 2:45–4:00 PM, in the same time slot as the MD technical sessions.
